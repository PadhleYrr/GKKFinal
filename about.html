About Us

Dedicated to supporting farmers with quality agricultural solutions

Our Story

Gumasta Krishi Kendra was established with a simple yet powerful mission: to empower farmers by providing access to premium agricultural products and expert guidance. Understanding the challenges faced by modern farming communities, we created a platform that combines quality products with farmer-centric service.

Our name itself reflects our commitment – "Gumasta Krishi" means "Commercial Agriculture," and "Kendra" means "Center" – we are a hub for agricultural excellence, built by farmers for farmers.

Our Mission

To provide farmers with access to trusted, high-quality agricultural products and solutions that enhance crop health, increase yield, and promote sustainable farming practices. We believe that every farmer deserves access to the best crop protection and soil health products available.

What We Offer

Our comprehensive product range includes:

  • Herbicides & Weedicides: Advanced weed control solutions for clean, healthy fields
  • Insecticides & Acaricides: Powerful pest management to protect your harvest
  • Fertilizers: Nutrient-rich products for optimal plant growth and yield
  • Bio-Stimulants & Soil Conditioners: Natural growth promoters and soil enhancement products
  • Adjuvants & Spreaders: Spray efficiency enhancers for better agrochemical performance
